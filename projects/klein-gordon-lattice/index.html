1. Theoretical Framework & Continuum Limit ($a \to 0$)

To bridge discrete analytical mechanics with classical field theory, we model a one-dimensional periodic network of $N$ particles of mass $m$ separated by equilibrium lattice spacing $a$. Expanding the nearest-neighbor interaction potential $U(a + \Delta q_n)$ ($\Delta q_n \equiv q_{n+1} - q_n$) to fourth order ($\kappa, \alpha, \beta$) and adding an on-site nonlinear potential $V_{\text{NL}}(q_n) = \frac{g_3}{3}q_n^3 + \frac{g_4}{4}q_n^4$ gives the discrete Lagrangian:

$$L(q,\dot{q}) = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\left[\frac{1}{2}m\dot{q}_n^2 - \frac{1}{2}\kappa(q_{n+1}-q_n)^2 - \frac{\alpha}{3}\kappa(q_{n+1}-q_n)^3 - \frac{\beta}{4}\kappa(q_{n+1}-q_n)^4 - \frac{1}{2}m\omega_0^2 q_n^2 - V_{\text{NL}}(q_n)\right]$$

Taking the continuum limit $N \to \infty$, $a \to 0$ at fixed physical length $l = Na$ with macroscopic density $\rho \equiv m/a$, tension $\tau \equiv \kappa a$, wave speed $c \equiv \sqrt{\tau/\rho} = a\sqrt{\kappa/m}$, mass parameter $\mu \equiv \omega_0/c$, scalar field $\phi(x_n, t) \equiv \sqrt{\rho}\,q_n(t)$, and rescaled couplings $\lambda_3 = g_3/(a\rho^{3/2})$, $\lambda_4 = g_4/(a\rho^2)$, the discrete Poisson bracket $\{\phi(x_n,t), \pi(x_m,t)\} = \delta_{nm}/a$ converges to $\{\phi(x,t), \pi(y,t)\} = \delta(x-y)$, and Euler–Lagrange equations yield the self-interacting Klein–Gordon equation:

$$\left(\frac{1}{c^2}\partial_t^2 - \partial_x^2 + \mu^2\right)\phi(x,t) = -\frac{1}{c^2}\frac{dV_{\text{int}}(\phi)}{d\phi}, \qquad V_{\text{int}}(\phi) = \frac{\lambda_3}{3}\phi^3 + \frac{\lambda_4}{4}\phi^4$$

In the infrared regime ($k_k a \ll 1$), the discrete normal-mode dispersion $\omega_k = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 + \frac{4\kappa}{m}\sin^2(k_k a/2)}$ converges to $\omega_{\text{KG}}(k_j) = c\sqrt{k_j^2 + \mu^2}$, with leading truncation discrepancy $\mathcal{O}(a^2\partial_x^4\phi)$. Time- and space-translation symmetries guarantee exact conservation of Noether energy $H[\phi,\pi]$ and linear momentum $P[\phi,\pi]$.

2. Symplectic & Spectral Numerical Architecture

On a periodic domain $x \in [0, l]$ ($l = 10.0, \rho = 1.0, \tau = 1.0, c = 1.0, \mu = 0.8$), both the discrete lattice accelerations (acc_disc) and the spectral Klein–Gordon field accelerations (acc_field) are evolved via a shared second-order symplectic Velocity Verlet integrator (verlet_step) subject to CFL stability $\Delta t = \nu_{\text{CFL}} a / c$: 

def acc_disc(q, m, kappa, w0, a=0., b=0., g3=0., g4=0.):
    dqr = np.roll(q, -1) - q
    dql = q - np.roll(q, 1)
    fl = kappa * (dqr - dql) - m * (w0**2) * q
    fs = -g3 * (q**2) - g4 * (q**3)
    fk = kappa * (a * (dqr**2 - dql**2) + b * (dqr**3 - dql**3))
    return (fl + fs + fk) / m

def acc_field(phi, k2, c, mu, l3=0., l4=0.):
    d2 = np.real(np.fft.ifft(-k2 * np.fft.fft(phi)))
    return (c**2) * d2 - (mu * c)**2 * phi - l3 * (phi**2) - l4 * (phi**3)

def verlet_step(u, v, dt, f_acc, *args):
    vm = v + 0.5 * dt * f_acc(u, *args)
    un = u + dt * vm
    vn = vm + 0.5 * dt * f_acc(un, *args)
    return un, vn

3. Quantitative Results

  • Spatiotemporal Energy Transport: For $N = 64$ anharmonic oscillators ($\alpha = 0.35, \beta = 0.25$), local energy density $h_n(t)$ propagates coherently across periodic boundaries at group velocity $v_g \approx c$ with a trailing dispersive wake induced by the mass parameter $\mu = 0.8$.
  • Nonlinear Harmonic Cascade & Noether Conservation: Initializing a pure excitation in the fundamental mode $k = 1$, anharmonic couplings break modal orthogonality and transfer energy $E_k(t) = Nm(|\dot{Q}_k(t)|^2 + \omega_k^2|Q_k(t)|^2)$ into higher harmonics ($k = 2, 3, 4$), while total Hamiltonian energy $H(t)$ remains conserved within relative fluctuations below $10^{-5}$.
  • Pointwise Convergence & $\mathcal{O}(a^2)$ Scaling: Under full self-interactions ($\lambda_3 = 0.25, \lambda_4 = 0.40$), refining the lattice from $N = 32$ ($a = 0.3125$) to $N = 128$ ($a = 0.0781$) eliminates coarse phase lag across multiple periodic crossings ($t \in [0, 12.5]$). Evaluating the global error $\epsilon_{L^2}(a,T) = \sqrt{a\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}|\sqrt{\rho}q_n(T) - \phi_{\text{field}}(x_n, T)|^2}$ at $T = 4.0$ across $N \in \{32, 64, 128, 256, 512\}$ yields a logarithmic slope of exactly $2.00$ ($\epsilon_{L^2} \propto a^2$) in both free and interacting regimes.
