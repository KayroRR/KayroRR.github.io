1. Problem Scope & Parallel Architecture

Large-scale physical simulations—ranging from Poisson potential solvers to gravitational $N$-body evolution—are bottlenecked by $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ pairwise force evaluations and inter-node memory latency. In the January 2025 CeNAT–CONARE HPC School Optimization Challenge, we re-engineered serial C/C++ solvers for distributed execution on multi-node SLURM partitions.

2. Optimization Techniques

  • Domain Decomposition & Non-Blocking MPI: Partitioned spatial grids across MPI ranks using halo-exchange buffers with non-blocking point-to-point communication (MPI_Isend / MPI_Irecv) to overlap communication with interior stencil computation.
  • Shared-Memory Threading & Cache Locality: Exploited OpenMP thread teams within compute nodes, enforcing first-touch NUMA memory alignment, loop tiling, and Newton's third-law symmetry to halve pairwise force operations.
  • Strong & Weak Scaling Benchmarks: Profiled wall-clock runtime and parallel efficiency across increasing core counts, achieving top execution speed and numerical accuracy among competing teams.
