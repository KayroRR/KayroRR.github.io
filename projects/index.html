COSMOLOGY · ALMA & STRONG LENSING01

Cosmological Implications of the $L'_{\text{CO}}\text{--FWHM}$ Relation for High-$z$ Dusty Star-Forming Galaxies

Evaluating the magnification-invariant $L'_{\text{CO}(1-0)}\text{--FWHM}$ scaling relation as a high-redshift ($z > 1$) dark energy probe by combining ALMA interferometric characterization of HARPAS-51 (CASA/CARTA) with population-level constraints on $\Lambda\text{CDM}$, $w\text{CDM}$, and CPL models.

EARLY-UNIVERSE COSMOLOGY · SDEs & PDEs02

Stochastic Inflation: Simulating Quantum Fluctuations as Coupled Fields

Monte Carlo Markov-chain simulations of coupled Hamiltonian Langevin equations (Euler–Maruyama) and 2D phase-space Fokker–Planck PDEs in conformal time $\tau$, capturing super-Hubble horizon freeze-out, Gaussian PDF convergence, and three dynamical regimes.

Python · Monte Carlo · Langevin & Fokker–PlanckRead Technical Overview →
CLASSICAL FIELD THEORY · CONTINUUM LIMIT03

Emergence of Classical Field Theory from Infinite Networks of Coupled Oscillators

Symplectic Velocity Verlet simulations of nonlinear anharmonic oscillator lattices in the continuum limit ($a \to 0$), demonstrating modal energy cascades from $k=1$, Noether conservation, and exact $\mathcal{O}(a^2)$ convergence toward the self-interacting Klein–Gordon equation.

Parallel PDE & $N$-Body Solver Optimization (MPI / OpenMP)

Hybrid shared- and distributed-memory parallelization of elliptic PDE and gravitational $N$-body solvers on CeNAT–CONARE's Kabré HPC cluster, achieving 1st Place in the 2025 HPC School Optimization Challenge.
