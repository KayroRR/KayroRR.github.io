01

Supervisor: Dr. Marianela Quirós Rojas

Dark Energy &
Strong Lensing

Designing a degeneracy-breaking dark energy probe by combining strong gravitational lens reconstruction with the magnification-invariant $L_{\text{CO}}\text{--FWHM}$ molecular-line scaling relation. Isolating intrinsic magnification factors ($\mu$) from mass-profile degeneracies constrains cosmological distance ratios and dynamical equations of state $(w_0, w_a)$.

02

Supervisor: Dr. Marlon Brenes

Stochastic Inflation
in de Sitter

Formulating the inflaton field as an open quantum system in de Sitter spacetime within the Starobinsky–Vennin stochastic inflation formalism. Implemented numerical solvers in Python for coupled Langevin equations (Euler–Maruyama trajectory ensembles) and Fokker–Planck PDEs to quantify primordial decoherence and non-Gaussian tails beyond slow-roll limits.

Surface-Code
Quantum Error Correction

Simulating topological surface-code circuits up to code distance $d = 7$ using Stim and QuTiP to benchmark logical error rate scaling and fault-tolerance thresholds under depolarizing circuit-level noise models.
